01 — Problem statement

Traditional classrooms fall short.

  • Rigid speed. One-size-fits-all teaching forces everyone through the same pace, whether they're ready or not.
  • Student disconnect. Slower learners fall behind while faster learners get bored waiting.
  • Lack of guidance. Real 1-on-1 help when you're stuck on a hard topic is rare and hard to schedule.
— sound familiar?
02 — AI solution

Meet StudyMate AI: your personal study assistant.

  • 24/7 personal tutor. Answers doubts instantly with simple, step-by-step explanations.
  • Adaptive difficulty. Automatically adjusts depth based on how you respond.
  • Targeted practice. Generates quick checks and summaries to fix weak spots.
